HOW TO PLAY

1 · Build: Choose a card, then tap an empty tile. Farms earn gold each year; defenders attack automatically.

2 · Prepare: Upgrade selected units, cast spells, and use your Champion Rally before battle.

3 · Fight: Tap End Year. Survive the enemy advance and protect your Citadel.

4 · Grow: Choose a campaign route after victory. Complete quests, defeat regional bosses, collect loot, and earn Legacy Crowns to unlock new regions.
